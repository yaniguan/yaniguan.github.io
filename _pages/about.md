<section class="about-section">
  <h2 class="about-section-title">Research</h2>
  <div class="about-rows">

    <div class="about-row">
      <div class="about-row-key">Electrochemical interfaces</div>
      <div class="about-row-val">
        First-principles models of double-layer structure, capacitance, and solvent
        organization at electrode–electrolyte interfaces — and the mechanisms behind
        metal dissolution and impurity-driven electrode aging.
      </div>
    </div>

    <div class="about-row">
      <div class="about-row-key">Electrolyte simulation</div>
      <div class="about-row-val">
        Polarizable force-field fitting and benchmarking for Na- and Li-ion chemistries,
        with molecular dynamics on HPC and Kubernetes for coordination structure,
        transport, and solvation thermodynamics.
      </div>
    </div>

    <div class="about-row">
      <div class="about-row-key">Molecular foundation models</div>
      <div class="about-row-val">
        Continued pretraining and post-training of open 2D/3D molecular backbones on a
        ~150M-molecule corpus, steering a general-chemistry foundation toward
        electrolyte-aware molecule and property representations.
      </div>
    </div>

    <div class="about-row">
      <div class="about-row-key">Multimodal chemistry</div>
      <div class="about-row-val">
        Vision–language models for optical chemical structure recognition
        (image→SMILES), and multimodal retrieval across text, figures, and structures
        that links a molecule to what the literature already knows about it.
      </div>
    </div>

    <div class="about-row">
      <div class="about-row-key">Learned dynamics</div>
      <div class="about-row-val">
        Representation learning on MD trajectories: a three-head encoder with
        latent-space pretraining objectives and a hierarchical decoder — a world model
        for microscopic molecular motion.
      </div>
    </div>

    <div class="about-row">
      <div class="about-row-key">Agentic co-scientists</div>
      <div class="about-row-val">
        Tool-using electrolyte and cell agents fine-tuned on reasoning traces distilled
        from multi-scale cell simulations, with leakage-safe benchmarks and default-deny
        governed job submission. Earlier tools:
        <a href="https://github.com/yaniguan/ChatDFT">ChatDFT</a> and
        <a href="https://github.com/yaniguan/ChemVisionAgent">ChemVisionAgent</a>.
      </div>
    </div>

  </div>
</section>
